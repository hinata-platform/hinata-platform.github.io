# Reports & dashboard

Hinata turns the work you already track into insight, with no extra data entry.

- The **dashboard** is your personal home screen with the overview.
- **Reports** are team analytics for planning and retrospectives.

![Hinata reports](/assets/img/shot-reports.png)
*Reports with burndown, distributions by state, priority and assignee, and PDF export.*

## Dashboard

The dashboard is the first thing you see after signing in. It is built around *today*:

- **Today's focus**: the issues that need your attention now.
- **Completion**: how much of your current work is done.
- **Weekly tracker**: your logged activity across the week.
- **Team ranking**: a light, motivating view of what the team contributes.

One glance tells you what to do next, on phone or desktop.

## Reports

The reports area combines issues, sprints and work logs into charts. Each one is interactive and reads the same live data as the rest of the app.

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Burndown** | Remaining work in the active sprint compared to the ideal line. Are you on pace to finish? |
| **Velocity** | Story points completed per sprint over time. Shows your team's sustainable throughput and is the basis for planning the next sprint. |
| **Cycle time** | How long issues take from start to done. A health check for lead time. |
| **Issues by state** | How work is spread across your workflow. Shows bottlenecks, such as a pile-up *In Review*. |
| **Issues by priority** | Where the urgent work sits. |
| **Issues by assignee** | Load across the team: who is overcommitted, who has room? |
| **Created vs. resolved** | Are you closing work faster than it arrives, or falling behind? |

!!! tip "Use velocity to plan capacity"
    After a few sprints, your average velocity is the reliable number to plan the next sprint's commitment against. It beats optimism by far.

### Export

Any report can be exported to **PDF** straight from the app, formatted for print. Use it for stakeholders, steering meetings or an archive.

!!! info "Reports respect access"
    Reports only include projects you can see. A member's charts follow their [team's project access](/en/projects-teams.html). Nothing leaks across visibility boundaries.
