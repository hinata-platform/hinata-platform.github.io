# Sharing an issue

Sometimes a person has to follow a single issue without being in its project or in a team that reaches it. The officer who ordered an eSIM from IT should be able to answer questions without seeing the whole IT project. For that you share exactly this issue with her. She then **takes part** in it.

## What participants can do

| Action | Member | Participant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read the issue, its history and attachments | yes | yes |
| Comment, reply, react, send a voice note, upload an attachment | yes | yes |
| Edit or delete their own comment | yes | yes |
| Change fields, move, archive, delete, clone | yes | no |
| Be assigned | yes | no |
| Log time | yes | no |
| Watch, export | yes | yes |
| Add more people | yes | no, only remove themselves |

![An issue as a participant sees it, with the note at the top](/assets/img/shot-sharing-participant.png)
*Lena takes part in INF-2 without seeing the project Infrastructure.*

Participants see of the project only what the issue needs to render: its name, key, states and labels. The project page, board, sprints, other issues, reports, the member list and logged time stay hidden from them. Linked issues and parent epics show only when they are shared with them too.

## Sharing an issue

1. Open the issue and tap **⋯** in the top right.
2. Choose **Share with…**.

![The ⋯ menu of an issue with Share with…](/assets/img/shot-sharing-menu.png)
*Share with… sits in the issue's ⋯ menu.*

3. Find the person in **Search for a person…** and tap them.

![The search in the Share dialog](/assets/img/shot-sharing-search.png)
*Tap the person in the results to share the issue with them.*

They now appear under **Participants**, with who added them and when. Below are the members who see the issue anyway.

![The Share dialog with two participants and the members below](/assets/img/shot-sharing-dialog.png)
*Participants with who added them, members underneath.* An issue can be shared with at most 25 people, and every active account of your organisation qualifies. People without an account keep reaching you through [email to ticket](/en/email-to-ticket).

Everyone who works in the project may share. Picking someone who is already a member changes nothing.

### Two shortcuts

- **Mention in a comment:** if you `@`-mention someone who cannot see the issue, a note appears below the comment field. **Share** opens the dialog with that person.
- **Assigning:** someone who cannot open the project cannot take the issue. Instead of an error, Hinata offers to share it with them.

## What the participant gets

They receive a notification "… shared INF-2 with you". The bell always rings; push and email follow their **Assignments** setting. From then on they watch the issue and hear about comments and changes like any watcher.

![Lena's notifications with the share and a new comment](/assets/img/shot-sharing-notification.png)
*The share and the first comment in Lena's notifications.*

They find the issue under **Watched**, in the **Issues** list and in search. At the top of the issue a note says that they take part and cannot see the project. Someone who is in no project at all gets a pointer to **Watched** on the empty Issues page.

![The Watched page with the shared issue at the top](/assets/img/shot-sharing-watched.png)
*Shared issues are listed under Watched.*

## Ending a share

- Members open **Share with…** and tap **Remove** next to the person.
- Participants tap **Leave** in the note at the top of the issue and confirm.

![The question before leaving an issue](/assets/img/shot-sharing-leave.png)
*Hinata asks once more before a participant leaves.*

Afterwards the person no longer sees the issue, their subscription ends and any mail still queued for them is dropped. An assignment stays as it was.

## What is recorded

Every share and every removal is in the issue's **History** ("… shared this issue with Lena Vogt") and in the organisation's audit log.

![The History tab of the issue with the share](/assets/img/shot-sharing-history.png)
*The share in the issue's history.* When an account is deleted it leaves every share. When an issue is moved, its participants stay.

!!! note "No marker on the issue"
    To members a shared issue looks like any other. The **Share with…** dialog shows who takes part.
