# Planning shifts

Shift planning organises events such as a trade fair stand, a club party or a duty roster. An event has shifts, and each shift needs a number of people. Helpers sign up themselves, planners assign. This page shows both, step by step. Background and operations are on the [Shift planning](/en/shifts.html) page.

!!! info "Switch it on first"
    Shift planning is off by default. Without it, **Shifts** is missing from the sidebar. How an admin switches it on is [below](#for-admins-switching-it-on).

## My shifts

Open **Shifts** in the sidebar, on a phone under **More**. You land on **Mine**.

![My shifts](/assets/img/shot-shifts-mine.png)
*Your next shift on the right, your shifts and open shifts in your projects on the left.*

- The **Next shift** card shows day, time, place and who is there with you.
- Below are your shifts by day, then open shifts that still need people.
- The honeycomb shows how full a shift is. Next to it are the number and a sign: a triangle means someone is missing, a tick means it is staffed, a lock means it is full.

![My shifts on a phone](/assets/img/shot-shifts-mobile-mine.png)
*On a phone your next shift is at the top.*

## Signing up and withdrawing

1. Open **Events** and then an event, or tap an open shift directly.
2. Tap **Sign up** on a shift.

![An event with its shifts](/assets/img/shot-shifts-event.png)
*The event with all its shifts by day. The top says how many places are taken.*

Tapping a shift opens its sheet with time, place and the people on it.

![The sheet of a shift](/assets/img/shot-shifts-sheet.png)
*The sheet of a shift. When you are on it, you see Withdraw here.*

If you cannot make it after all, choose **Withdraw**. Hinata asks once, then your place is free again.

!!! tip "Not in two places at once"
    If a shift overlaps one you are already on, Hinata refuses the sign-up. This holds across projects too.

## Joining the waiting list

When a shift is full, it offers **Join waiting list**.

![Waiting list on a full shift](/assets/img/shot-shifts-waitlist.png)
*The grill shift is full. You are first on its waiting list.*

- When a place frees up, you move up automatically and get a notification.
- **Mine** then says **A place opened up for you, please confirm by …**. Tap **Confirm**.
- **Leave waiting list** takes you off again.

## Being assigned

When a planner assigns you, the shift appears as **Assigned** in your list. **Confirm** accepts it, **Decline** gives the place back.

## The calendar

**Calendar** shows every shift of your projects as a week or a month.

![The week in the calendar](/assets/img/shot-shifts-calendar-week.png)
*The week opens an hour before its first shift. Signs and numbers as in the list.*

- **All projects** filters to one project.
- **Understaffed only** shows only shifts that still need people.
- **Month** switches to the month. A dot means your own shift, a ring means understaffed.

![The month in the calendar](/assets/img/shot-shifts-calendar-month.png)
*In the month each day shows only its marks. Tapping opens the day.*

On a phone the calendar shows one day with the week strip above it. The month name at the top opens the menu with view, week or month, today and the filters.

![The calendar on a phone with its menu](/assets/img/shot-shifts-mobile-calendar-menu.png)
*The menu behind the month name.*

## For planners

Project leads and team admins can plan, and so can everyone listed as a planner in the project.

### Creating an event

1. Open **Events** and tap **+ Event**.
2. Choose the **Project**, enter **Name**, **Start**, **End** and **Place**.
3. Tap **Create**.

![The editor for a new event](/assets/img/shot-shifts-event-editor.png)
*An event needs a project, a name, a start and an end.*

**More details** holds the time zone and the switch **Members may sign up**. When it is off, only planners assign.

### Series

For an event that repeats, choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly or **Custom rule** under **Repeat**.

![Repeat](/assets/img/shot-shifts-repeat.png)
*The choices under Repeat.*

![Custom rule](/assets/img/shot-shifts-repeat-custom.png)
*A custom rule sets the interval, the weekdays and when the series ends.*

!!! warning "Start and end are for one occurrence"
    In a series, start and end are the times of a single occurrence, for example Tuesday 10:00 to 18:00. When the series stops is set under **Custom rule** at **End**. A single occurrence lasts at most 14 days.

When you later change an occurrence, Hinata asks: **Only this one** or **All future ones**.

### Adding shifts

On an event's page, **+ Shift** adds a shift. The calendar is quicker:

1. Open **Calendar** in the week.
2. Drag with the mouse across the time the shift should last. On a phone, press and hold, then drag.
3. When you let go, the editor opens with exactly that time in the right event.
4. Enter a name, choose **Needed** and, if you like, an **Upper limit**, then **Save**.

![The editor after dragging in the calendar](/assets/img/shot-shifts-calendar-sweep.png)
*Dragged on Thursday from 9:30 to 11:30. The event is already chosen.*

If several events cover the span, Hinata asks which one. If none does, it offers **New event at this time**.

Without dragging, use **+** in the calendar: **New event** or **Add shift**.

![The plus in the calendar](/assets/img/shot-shifts-calendar-add.png)
*The plus next to Month, on a phone at the top next to the bell.*

**Copy to more days** in the editor adds the same shift to several days at once.

### Assigning and taking people off

1. Tap **Assign** on a shift.
2. Search for the person and tap them. **Sign me up** is at the top.

![The person picker when assigning](/assets/img/shot-shifts-assign.png)
*People already on the shift are greyed out.*

In a shift's sheet, the **x** next to a name takes the person off. A place that frees up goes to the first person on the waiting list automatically.

### Moving, cancelling, deleting

- In the calendar, drag a block somewhere else. Hinata asks once.
- **More** on an event's page offers **Edit**, **Cancel** and **Delete**.

![The menu of an event](/assets/img/shot-shifts-event-menu.png)
*The More menu on an event's page.*

### Exporting the shift plan

1. On an event's page tap **More**, then **Export shift plan**.
2. Choose **PDF**, **Word** or **Spreadsheet**.
3. **With names** puts people's names in the file. Only planners see this switch.

![Export shift plan](/assets/img/shot-shifts-export.png)
*The export as PDF, Word or spreadsheet.*

### Naming planners

A project lead names planners in the **Project settings** under **Shift planning** with **Add planner**.

![Planners in the project settings](/assets/img/shot-shifts-planners.png)
*Planners assign and add shifts but cannot change the project settings.*

## Shifts in your own calendar

Your shifts can show up in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Notifications**, at the bottom **Shifts in your own calendar**.
2. Tap **Create subscription address**.
3. Tap **Open in calendar** or copy the address into your calendar.

![Shifts in your own calendar](/assets/img/shot-shifts-feed.png)
*An active subscription. Creating a new one makes the old address stop working.*

!!! tip "Apple Calendar"
    When subscribing, choose **On My Mac** as the location if your Hinata is only reachable on your own network. With iCloud, Apple fetches the address from outside.

The address is shown only once. The subscription holds only your own shifts.

## For admins: switching it on

1. Open **Admin**, then **Platform**.
2. Switch on **Shift planning**.

![The switch in the admin area](/assets/img/shot-shifts-admin-switch.png)
*The Shift planning switch under Platform behaviour.*

Shift planning needs MongoDB as a replica set. Without one, a note takes the place of the switch. More on the [Shift planning](/en/shifts.html) page.
